 PART 1 - WHERE WE STARTED
=====================================================================

 The first review of escapeRoom.js, index.html and a4.css found
 17 issues:

   #1   The vault could never be unlocked. The game couldn't be won.
   #2   "Begin Your Journey" skipped the player details form. Everyone
        was "Player" / "Team Mindful" / Player 1, so everyone got the
        same secret word.
   #3   The details form stayed on screen during the whole game.
   #4   Typing a space in the name box skipped a breathing cycle.
   #5   The Room 2 colour picker did nothing.
   #6   The Room 2 wind animation was never visible.
   #7   A wrong brick answer gave no feedback.
   #8   No pop-up after breaking the wall.
   #9   The drawing line jumps if the mouse leaves the drawing area and
        comes back.
   #10  Drawing doesn't work on touch screens.
   #11  The breathing countdown says "1 seconds".
   #12  The secret word overlapped the vault picture.
   #13  The Room 2 colours were pure neon and clashed with the design.
   #14  Colour variable names don't match their colours.
   #15  Unused variables (gameStarted, game.title).
   #16  Duplicate / unused CSS rules.
   #17  The Player dropdown and the secret word list had to be kept in
        sync by hand.

 Added along the way, from your requests:
   #18  Details page bigger and more centred.
   #19  Game must not start without a name and team name.
   #20  Canvas colour different from last time.
   #21  Same title on every page.
   #22  Fixed colour pairs for Room 2 instead of a colour picker.
